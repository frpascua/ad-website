Brand & Style

This design system is built upon the principles of Radical Functional Minimalism. It prioritizes clarity, accessibility, and trust over decorative trends. The brand personality is serious, authoritative, and helpful, designed to provide a seamless interface between the citizen and the state.

The aesthetic is characterized by a "content-first" approach:

  • Minimalism: Use of white space as a structural tool to reduce cognitive load.
  • High Contrast: Every element is optimized for legibility and clear visual hierarchy.
  • Utilitarianism: Every design choice serves a functional purpose; there are no unnecessary embellishments.
  • Accessibility: Compliance with WCAG 2.2 AAA standards is the baseline for all interactions.

Colors

The palette is intentionally limited to ensure critical information is never obscured.

  • Primary Blue (#1D70B8): Used for actionable items like links and primary navigation elements.
  • Secondary Green (#00703C): Reserved for "Start" buttons and success states, signifying progression.
  • Neutral/Text (#0B0C0C): A near-black used for all primary text to ensure maximum contrast against white.
  • Background Grey (#F3F2F1): Used for site-wide footers and secondary content areas to provide subtle visual separation.
  • Focus Yellow (#FFDD00): A critical functional color used exclusively for focus states to ensure high visibility for keyboard navigation.

Typography

The system utilizes Inter as a highly legible, web-safe alternative to GDS Transport. The typographic scale is aggressive to ensure a clear information hierarchy.

  • Headings: Use bold weights (700) for all headings. The headline-xl is reserved for the main page title (H1).
  • Body Text: The standard reading size is body-lg (19px) to improve accessibility for users with visual impairments.
  • Line Height: Line heights are tight for headings but generous for body text to maintain a comfortable reading rhythm.
  • Links: All links should be underlined by default to distinguish them from standard text, except in primary navigation components.

Layout & Spacing

The design system follows a Fixed Grid philosophy for desktop to ensure content remains readable and focused.

  • Main Container: Max-width is capped at 960px to maintain optimal line lengths for reading.
  • Single Column Focus: Forms and informational content should primarily follow a single-column layout to guide the user downward without distraction.
  • The 5px Scale: All spacing (margins, padding) is derived from a 5px base unit, typically used in increments of 10, 15, 20, 30, and 50px.
  • Breakpoints:
    • Mobile: 0px - 640px (Margins: 15px). Content reflows to 100% width.
    • Desktop: 641px+ (Margins: 30px). Content centers within the 960px container.

Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids physical metaphors, shadows, and blurs. Depth is conveyed entirely through Tonal Layers and Bold Borders.

  • Flatness: Surfaces are flat. No box-shadows are used to indicate elevation.
  • High-Contrast Outlines: Interactive elements like input fields use solid 2px borders (#0b0c0c).
  • Focus States: When an element is focused via keyboard, it receives a 3px solid black border and a thick #FFDD00 (Focus Yellow) background or outline. This is the only exception where "glow" or "outer strokes" are simulated.
  • Separation: Horizontal rules (1px or 2px solid #B1B4B6) are used to separate logical sections of content.

Shapes

The shape language is strictly Sharp (0px).

Every UI element—including buttons, input fields, and tags—must have square corners. This reinforces the serious, institutional, and stable nature of the design system. Rounded corners are seen as decorative and are therefore excluded.

Components

Buttons

  • Primary ("Start now"): Large, green (#00703C), white text, bold weight. Includes a white chevron icon > to the right.
  • Secondary: Grey (#F3F2F1) background with black text and a bottom border shadow (2px #0b0c0c) to indicate pressability without using actual elevation.

Form Inputs

  • Text Fields: 2px solid black borders. Labels must be placed above the input, never as placeholder text.
  • Checkboxes & Radios: Large (40x40px) hit areas for accessibility. Selected states use a solid black fill.

Links

  • Back Links: Placed at the top left of the content area. Simple text "Back" with a subtle underline and a small left-pointing arrow.

Banners

  • Header: A solid #0B0C0C bar at the very top, featuring the "Crown" logo and the site title in white.
  • Phase Banner: A small tag (e.g., "ALPHA" or "BETA") used at the top of the content to indicate the service status.

Cards & Lists

  • Details Component: A summary text with a small triangle icon that expands to show more information.
  • Lists: Bulleted lists use square markers; numbered lists are used for sequential steps.
